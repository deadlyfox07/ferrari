COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Is the LaFerrari still available to purchase?

Production of the 499 units has concluded. However, pre-owned examples occasionally become available through Ferrari Classiche and authorised dealers. Our team can assist in sourcing these rare opportunities.

What is required to be considered for ownership?

Historically, Ferrari personally approved each buyer based on their existing relationship with the brand. For pre-owned examples, our specialist team will guide you through the authentication and transfer process.

Can I configure a bespoke specification?

Through the Ferrari Tailor Made programme, extensive personalisation was offered on each LaFerrari. For pre-owned examples, we can discuss cosmetic and interior restoration to your preferences.

What after-sales support is available?

Ferrari Classiche provides comprehensive certification, restoration, and maintenance support for all LaFerrari owners. Spare parts and specialist technicians are available through the global Ferrari network.

Is the LaFerrari Aperta different mechanically?

The Aperta shares the identical 963CV hybrid powertrain as the coupe, with aerodynamic recalibration for the open configuration. Only 210 were produced, making it even rarer than the coupe.

What is the current market value?

LaFerrari values have appreciated significantly since launch. Current market values for well-documented examples can range between $3.5M and $6M USD, depending on specification and provenance.
